Six marks, built as objects

These are real 3D renders: physically based metals and clearcoat, glass, extruded geometry and an environment map for reflections. Every mark keeps the product's colour code: mint for a recalled memory, amber for one marked wrong.

How to pick. For the website hero and video, the chrome spiral and the glass orb show the most depth. For a mark that also works as a flat favicon, the spiral pebble and the extruded h reduce best to one colour.
