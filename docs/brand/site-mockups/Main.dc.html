Memory for coding agents

Your agents forget what went wrong. Hippo keeps the lesson.

Hippo turns failed commands, decisions and compaction snapshots into memories your agents recall next time. What gets used grows stronger. What is marked wrong drops out. It runs locally, next to Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Copilot.

Captures without asking

Failed tool calls become error memories. Compaction saves a task snapshot and restores it after. You see a line when it happens.

Forgets on purpose

Unused memories fade into a dormant store you can restore. Wrong ones are suppressed and can be banned for good.

Shows its working

Every eval is registered before it runs, and losses are published beside wins. Your own before and after comes from your own telemetry.
